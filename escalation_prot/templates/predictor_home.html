The predictor turns the escalation framework into something you can poke at. You compose or step through a support conversation, and for each message it reports the linguistic and semantic features the analysis is built on — sentiment, deflection and bureaucratic language, passive voice, negation, intent and frustration terms, and so on.

Once a conversation has at least two turns, it also reads the whole thread through the trained XGBoost model and shows the probability of an ineffective resolution, broken down by each feature's SHAP contribution — how much, and in which direction, it pushed that prediction.

Two ways to use it

FROM THE START — open with a real dataset message or write your own, then build the thread turn by turn.
JUMP INTO THE MIDDLE — a real thread is drawn at random; you see the first three turns, write the agent's reply, and then reveal what actually happened to compare against.

Read with caution

Everything here describes association in one dataset, not causation, and the model was trained on completed threads — short or in-progress conversations are extrapolation and are flagged as such. See the full disclaimer below.
